About Us

A neighbourhood counter, still doing it the old way

Tucked into Poonthottam, Agarathirumalam, Sri Raghavendra Iyengar Bakery is the kind of shop where the smell of the morning bake reaches the street before the shutters are even fully up.

Like the Iyengar bakeries this region is known for, the shelves stay stocked with everyday bread, biscuits and snacks, along with cakes made to order for birthdays, anniversaries and celebrations.
