About Sri Raghavendra Iyengar Bakery

A local bakery serving freshly prepared bakery products and celebration cakes in Poonthottam.

Who We Are

Baking the everyday basics, the traditional way

Sri Raghavendra Iyengar Bakery is a neighbourhood bakery and cake shop in Poonthottam, Agarathirumalam, Tamil Nadu — the kind of shop people stop at on the way to work for a rusk and a cup of tea, and the kind they turn to when a birthday needs a cake on short notice.

Like the Iyengar bakeries this region is known for, we've built our name on consistency rather than trends: the same recipes, the same freshness, day after day.

Full history, founding year, and more of our story will be added here once shared by the bakery.
